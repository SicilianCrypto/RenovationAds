Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The short version: we collect the details you enter so we can follow up about your renovation project and book your consultation. We don't sell your information. We also use Meta (Facebook) advertising tools to measure how our ads perform. You can ask us to see, correct, or delete your information at any time.

Who we are

This page is operated by RenovationAds ("we", "us"), based in Montréal, Québec. We connect homeowners who are planning a kitchen or bathroom renovation with renovation professionals who can help.

What we collect

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • Your city and details about your project (scope, budget range, timeline, and whether you own your home)
  • Any notes you choose to write
  • Your booking details if you schedule a consultation
  • Basic technical information about your visit (such as the page you viewed and your device and browser type), collected through cookies and similar technologies

Why we collect it

  • To review your request and follow up with you about your project
  • To book and confirm your consultation
  • To pass your request to a renovation professional who can help with your project
  • To measure and improve the performance of our advertising

Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal information. We use trusted service providers to run this page, and they handle your information on our behalf:

  • Formspree, which receives your form answers and sends them to us
  • Cal.com and Google (Calendar and Meet), which handle bookings and video meeting links
  • Vercel, which hosts this website
  • Meta Platforms (Facebook and Instagram), for advertising measurement (see below)

When you ask for a consultation, we may also share your request details with the renovation professional who will contact you. Some of these providers store information outside Québec, including in the United States.

Advertising and cookies

We use the Meta Pixel on this page, but only if you click "Accept" in our cookie banner. If you decline, it never loads. When it's on, it records visits and, when you book a consultation, lets us tell Meta that a booking happened. We use this to measure our ads and to show our ads to people who may be interested.

You can change your choice at any time using the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of our form page. You can also limit tracking through your browser's privacy settings, and through your ad preferences in your Facebook and Instagram account settings.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for up to 24 months after our last contact with you. After that, we delete it or make it anonymous, unless the law requires us to keep it longer. You can also ask us to delete it sooner.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we hold about you and give you a copy
  • Correct anything that's wrong or out of date
  • Delete your information or stop using it
  • Withdraw your consent to how we use it

Contact us at the address below. We'll respond as quickly as we can and within the time required by law.

Contact us

Changes to this policy
